# 005.01 — Narrowing conversions: brace initialization rejects them: compiler comparison

**g++ vs clang++ (g++ 16.2.1, clang++ 23.1.1, `-std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra`, `-O0` and `-O2`).** Both compilers reject the program, with different text. g++ reports two errors, `narrowing conversion of '300' from 'int' to 'uint8_t' {aka 'unsigned char'} [-Wnarrowing]` (line 9) and `narrowing conversion of '3.5e+0' from 'double' to 'int' [-Wnarrowing]` (line 10). clang++ reports two errors, `constant expression evaluates to 300 which cannot be narrowed to type 'std::uint8_t' (aka 'unsigned char') [-Wc++11-narrowing]` and `type 'double' cannot be narrowed to 'int' in initializer list [-Wc++11-narrowing]`, plus two extra warnings (`-Wliteral-conversion` for 3.5 to 3, `-Wconstant-conversion` for 300 to 44) that g++ does not print. The result is the same at `-O0` and `-O2`.
