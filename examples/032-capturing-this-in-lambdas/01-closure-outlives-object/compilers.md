# 032.01 — Capturing `this` in lambdas: the closure outlives the object: compiler comparison

**g++ vs clang++ (g++ 16.2.1, clang++ 23.1.1, `-std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra` and `-fsanitize=address`, `-O0` and `-O2`).** Both compilers warn about the implicit `this` capture through `[=]` (line 12), with different flags: g++ `-Wdeprecated` (`implicit capture of 'this' via '[=]' is deprecated in C++20`), clang++ `-Wdeprecated-this-capture`. That warning is about the deprecated capture, not about the dangling pointer. Without a sanitizer both print a garbage `clicks=` value; ASan reports `heap-use-after-free` under both compilers at every level.
